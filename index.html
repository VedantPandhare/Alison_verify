Course Information

Learn the best methods and principles required to conduct and compile effective research with this free online course.

This course introduces scientific research and compares the different types, steps, methods and criteria used in a successful research project. It also explains research ethics and misconduct and shows you how to formulate a clear research problem.

You will learn how to design your research, select appropriate samples and choose effective methods of collecting data. The course also outlines how to process, analyse and present research findings clearly and accurately.

Finally, the course explains how to bring each stage of your work together in an effective research proposal. These practical research skills can help you plan and communicate a structured, ethical and reliable study.
